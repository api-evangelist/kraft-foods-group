# Kraft Foods Group (kraft-foods-group)
Kraft Foods Group was a North American grocery business that produced packaged food and beverages including cheese, refrigerated meals, refreshment beverages, coffee, and convenient meals before merging with H.J. Heinz to form The Kraft Heinz Company in 2015.
